Statutory Architectural Framework

Four Pillars of National Aviation Price Surveillance

Engineered in strict compliance with the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation guidelines, IMF CPI Manual 2020, and DGCA Air Transport Regulations.

Pillar 01

Multi-Source RFC 9309 Ingestion

Decentralized high-throughput crawlers spanning 4 active live scrapers and 7 statutory validation portals. Compliant with RFC 9309 robots.txt standards with zero bot-blocking.

Auditable Data Provenance
Pillar 02

Laspeyres Index Mathematics

Rigorous econometric formulation weighting city-pairs by annual passenger traffic (Wr). Mathematically isolates pure price inflation from volume distortions.

It = (Sum Pt*Q0 / Sum P0*Q0) * 100
Pillar 03

4-Part Fare Decomposition

Deconstructs aggregate ticket prices into Base Fare, Fuel Surcharge (YQ/YR), Airport UDF/PSF/DF, and statutory GST (5% Economy / 12% Business).

Total = Base + Fuel + Airport + GST
Pillar 04

ML Nowcast & Calamity Alerts

Ensemble XGBoost and LightGBM forward models projecting 7-day to 30-day index trajectories. Automated alert triggers detect route price-gouging during climatic emergencies.

Trained on 1,82,000+ Records
Institutional Governance

Statutory Alignment & National Coordination

Seamlessly feeding official macroeconomic indicators to regulatory authorities and apex statistical bodies across India.

Ministry of Statistics (MoSPI)

Direct integration with National Statistical Office (NSO) Consumer Price Index releases under COICOP Group 07.3.3. Replaces manual monthly surveys with auditable high-frequency feeds.

Directorate General of Civil Aviation

Powers the DGCA Tariff Monitoring Unit with real-time surge triggers and price-band ceiling compliance across all commercial scheduled operators in Indian airspace.

Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Feeds the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) with daily provisional transportation nowcasts and ATF price-transmission elasticity metrics for precision inflation targeting.
